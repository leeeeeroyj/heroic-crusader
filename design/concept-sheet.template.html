Heroic Crusader

Take the squire's vow. Walk a storybook kingdom gone dim, win turn-based fights against creatures soured by the Gloam, and relight the four beacons before the last light goes out.

The journey screen

Live mockup at the game's real resolution (416×228, 16 px tiles). A demo squire walks the kingdom, gets ambushed, fights side-on, visits towns and takes on the Meadow warden. Click the screen and take over; the demo resumes after a few idle seconds. Sprites you pick below swap in here.

The squire & the townsfolk

One hero, named by you. The squire flips to face the way it walks and always faces right in battle, so pick art that mirrors well. Townsfolk stand still in towns and talk when you press A beside them.

Soured creatures

Nine families, seventeen creatures: later regions bring recoloured, tougher kin. They aren't killed. A beaten creature shakes off the Gloam and runs home. Drawn at 2× in battle.

Beacon wardens & the Gloam

Each beacon sits at the bottom of a dungeon behind a warden, drawn at 3×. You can't run from a warden. The Gloam waits in its Hollow, sealed behind a wall that only all four beacons can burn away.

Things you find

Map objects and HUD icons. Icons show at half size (8 px) in menus and the top bar, still pixel-exact because the game renders at 4×.

How the crusade plays

Walk, fight, growFights start at random as you walk. Each kind of ground has its own odds: roads are safest, marsh and ruins the worst. Creatures get tougher the farther you go from Brightkeep.
Fight · Spell · Item · FleeYou act, then each foe in turn. Up to three foes at once. Fleeing works about two times in three and costs you the turn when it fails. You can never flee a warden.
Valor timingA ring closes on the target as each blow lands. Press A as it closes: your strike does 1.5×, or a foe's blow does half. Miss the beat and nothing is lost. It can be switched off.
LV 1 → 15Beating creatures earns Valor (experience) and gold. Valor raises your level: more HP, MP, attack and defence, and a new spell every level or two.
ShrinesEvery town has a shrine. The keeper saves your journey, cures poison, and is where you wake if you fall, with half your gold gone. Inns refill HP and MP for a few gold.
Four beaconsMeadow, Marsh, Snow and Ruins. Beat a warden and its beacon burns: the colour comes back to that region, the fog lifts and fights there come a third less often.
Dark belowDungeons are dark. Iron keys from chests or the hermit open locked doors and are used up. Herbs, keys and the Homeward spell are how you get back out.
About an hourOne overworld, three towns and a hermit's hut, four beacon dungeons and the Hollow. One save slot in your browser.

From the 1986 original

Heroic Crusader borrows the shape of Chunsoft and Enix's Dragon Quest: one hero, a tile map, menu battles and a long walk to a final foe. The world, story, names, characters, monsters, art and music are new.

Kept
  • One hero against the world, levels and gold
  • Random fights by terrain, harder farther from home
  • Fight, Spell, Item, Flee menus
  • Spells learned by level, herbs, keys and locked doors
  • Dark dungeons and a light spell
  • Lose half your gold when you fall
  • A final foe with a second form
Changed
  • Storybook kingdom; four beacons to relight, not relics to gather
  • Side-view battles with the hero on screen
  • Up to three foes at a time
  • Creatures are freed, not slain
  • Save at any town's shrine
  • No command menu on the map: A talks, searches, opens
Added
  • Valor timing for strikes and guards
  • Foes flash before they act
  • Regions regain colour as beacons burn
  • A short quest line in the menu
  • Controller and touch from day one
  • Sparks, shake and procedural sound
